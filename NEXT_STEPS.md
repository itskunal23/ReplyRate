# Next steps

Only the first item matters until it has an answer.

## 1. Prove people want the Ghosted Report

Plan: [Experiments](docs/04-validation/EXPERIMENTS.md), test 1. Two weeks, 10 Reels.

| Result | Reports made | Shared | Added company names |
|---|---|---|---|
| Pass | 500 or more | 10% or more | 20% or more |
| Fail | under 100 | under 3% | |

Before it can start, you need to decide or do these. Each one needs your go-ahead:

- [ ] Check that a name is free: ReplyRate is unchecked as a brand, domain and social handle.
- [ ] Buy a domain (about $12 a year) and choose where the free page is hosted.
- [ ] Create the Instagram, TikTok and YouTube accounts.
- [ ] Write and film the first 10 Reels using the [playbook](docs/06-operating-model/REELS_PLAYBOOK.md).
- [ ] Get short legal advice on the invoice wording and on handling employer names.

Also decide whether to start the job-post collector early. Repost history only builds up from the day collection starts, so waiting delays it. See the [spec](docs/00-thesis/PRODUCT_SPEC.md#8-posting-history).

## 2. If it passes, test the tool

Test 2 in the same file: at least 200 people log 5 or more applications and at least 10 pay. Then pick the first group: laid-off US tech workers, about 200 companies.

## 3. If it fails

Write down why in the [results log](docs/04-validation/RESULTS_LOG.md). Then go to the [backups](docs/01-opportunities/OPPORTUNITIES.md#backups).
