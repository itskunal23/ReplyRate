## Ghost jobs

Estimates run from about 1 in 5 to 1 in 3 postings. There is no official figure. Do not state one as fact.

## Proof that people pay near this problem

Teal about $4.2M and Jobright about $1M revenue in 2024 ([Teal](https://www.cbinsights.com/company/teal-1/financials), [Jobright](https://www.cbinsights.com/company/jobright/financials); CB Insights estimates). That is adjacent evidence. No one has paid for ReplyRate.
