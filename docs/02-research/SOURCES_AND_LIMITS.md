# Sources and limits

## What this research is

Web searches on 2026-11-08, read as result summaries rather than full pages. Links are in [Pain points](PAIN_POINTS.md) and [Competitors](COMPETITORS.md).

## What it is not

- **Reddit and Threads could not be read.** Those searches were blocked. Instagram returned nothing usable. Social demand and emotional sentiment were not measured, though that was asked for.
- No job seeker has been interviewed.
- Nobody has paid for this. Pay evidence is by analogy.
- Many sources are vendors selling something near this problem.
- Psychology claims are from memory, not checked against studies.
- Google's Gmail review cost is from memory.
- Scores are judgment. The 90/100 bar was not met by anything.
- The name ReplyRate has not been checked for availability or trademark.

## Before quoting publicly

Open the original, confirm the number, sample and date, and link it in the Reel's caption.
