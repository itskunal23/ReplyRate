# Competitors

Reviewed through search summaries only. Pricing and features can be out of date.

| Competitor | What they do | Gap ReplyRate aims at | Threat |
|---|---|---|---|
| LinkedIn, Indeed | Job listings | Revenue comes from employers, so a low incentive to show who ignores applicants | Medium |
| Glassdoor | Reviews and salaries | Opinions, not reply outcomes. Its give-to-get model is the precedent | Medium |
| Teal, Simplify, Jobright | Tracking, résumé help, matching | They hold tracking data and could add reply rates | **High** |
| Auto-apply tools | Send more applications | Make the noise worse. We reduce wasted applications | Low |
| [Ghost Job Checker](https://chromewebstore.google.com/detail/ghost-job-checker/lkojpkppkconnpiknflmkigafplcedkc), [GhostJob](https://chromewebstore.google.com/detail/ghostjob-ghost-job-detect/clbbopifmidceplphamfdfapgacgbhnd), [Subspace](https://extensionauditor.com/scan/subspace-ghost-job-scanner-pffpilfklpmjcjgaepljilhbciiojndn) | Guess if a posting is real from listing signals | Guess at the posting. We measure real outcomes | Medium |
| [ApplyBase](https://applybase.app/) | Application tracking, Sankey view | Personal stats, not shared | Low |
| JobHunt (open source) | Tracker | Same | Low |
| [Orbyt Social](https://orbytsocial.com/) | Social for job seekers | Community without outcome data | Low |
| [InstantHire](https://instanthire.online/) | Hiring tool | Different side | Low |

## The real defense

Nothing stops a copy of the features. The only defenses are: reaching the outcome data first, the public Ghost Index as a brand, and the rule that employers cannot buy a score, which ad-funded rivals may not be able to promise.
