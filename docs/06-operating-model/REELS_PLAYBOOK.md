# Reels playbook

Human-directed, AI-assisted, not fully AI-generated. Fully generated channels carry platform policy risk and earn little.

## Three formats

| Format | Example | Rule |
|---|---|---|
| Corporate translation | "'We'll keep your résumé on file.' Translation: ..." | Never name a company |
| Psychology | Why silence hurts more than a no | Cite the study in the caption |
| Our own data | "This month 61% got no reply" | Only when real, with sample and rule stated |

## The loop

Reel → Ghosted Report → card shared → someone new sees it → new Reel from the numbers.

## Each Reel ends the same way

"Make your Ghosting Invoice. Link in bio." One action.

## What we measure

Reports made and people who log, by Reel. Not views.

## Do not

Name companies without samples and legal advice. Use fake testimonials or engagement. Show invented data as real. Exploit people's distress for reach.
