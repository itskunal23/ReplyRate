# Opportunities

Scores are judgment. No idea reached 90/100 and none has real demand evidence specific to it.

## How the choice was made

The first pass screened 30 opportunities for an autonomous B2B data business and picked FirstTab. That full table is in the [FirstTab report](../05-investment-committee/history/FIRSTTAB_REPORT_2026-11-08.md). The founder then redirected toward a consumer tool people badly want, spread through Reels. The second pass ranked current pain points and tested ideas against that goal.

## Lead

**ReplyRate.** About 7/10 (72/100) as a tool, ease of starting 9/10, about 66/100 for the full network version today.

Why it won:

- The job black hole was the strongest pain found, and it directly suits the founder's chosen market. [V]
- The launch needs no data, no account and little code.
- It fits Reels: corporate language and ghosting are naturally shareable.
- Outcome data can become an advantage that a résumé tool does not have.

Why it is not higher: the data starts empty, Teal or Simplify could copy it, users leave when hired, and reports have selection bias.
