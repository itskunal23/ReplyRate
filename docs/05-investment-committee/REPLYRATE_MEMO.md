# ReplyRate: investment memo

Date: 2026-11-08. **Verdict: conditional go, for the two-week Ghosted Report test only. Do not build the full product yet.**

## Recommendation

Spend under $100 and two weeks finding out whether people make and share the card. Everything else depends on that.

## Why it is worth testing

- Strongest pain found in the research: the job black hole. [V]
- Easy to start, 9/10: a one-page tool with no data or account.
- The card and the corporate-language Reels are naturally shareable. [H]
- If it works, the outcome data is an advantage a résumé tool does not already hold. [H]

## Why it could fail

| Risk | Severity |
|---|---|
| Empty at the start: no data, no reason to log | High |
| Teal or Simplify copies it | High |
| Users leave when hired | Medium, by design |
| Selection bias makes rates unfair | High |
| Legal complaints from named employers | High |
| Reels do not perform | Medium |

Full list: [Risks](../07-decisions/RISKS.md).

## Score

About 7/10 (72/100) for the tool, about 66/100 for the network version. Judgment, not measurement. Getting to 10/10 requires the [scoreboard](../04-validation/EXPERIMENTS.md#the-1010-scoreboard), and above all proof that it gets people more human replies.

## What we do not know

Whether anyone wants this. See [Sources and limits](../02-research/SOURCES_AND_LIMITS.md). Reddit, Threads and Instagram sentiment could not be read, and no job seeker has been interviewed.

## Decision rule

Pass test 1: go to test 2. Fail: use a [backup](../01-opportunities/OPPORTUNITIES.md#backups). Do not rescue a failed test by adding features.
