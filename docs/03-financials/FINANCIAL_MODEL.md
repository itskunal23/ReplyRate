# Financial model

Every figure here is arithmetic on assumptions. No revenue exists. Pre-tax, before refunds unless noted.

## Price ladder

| Product | Price | Stage | Rule |
|---|---|---|---|
| Ghosted Report | Free | 1 | Always free |
| Company reply rates | Free | 1 | Open to everyone at 30 reports |
| Next 10 best bets with reply guarantee | $9 per pack | 1 | Log all 10. Money back if none gets a human reply in 30 days. One claim per person. Claimants' outcomes stay out of public rates unless verified |
| Pay when hired | $49 once, optional | 1 | No income share |
| Hardship route | Free | 1 | Log 20 applications, get the next 10 best bets free |
| Employer insights and Reply Pledge badge | About $299 per role a month | 3 | Never changes a score |

## Unit math

**Pay when hired.** 1,000 users × 40% hired [A] × 25% pay [A] × $49 = **$4,900** [C].

**$9 guarantee.** With a 20% reply chance on each of 10 picks [A], the chance none reply is 0.8^10 ≈ 10.7% [C]. Expected refund about **$0.96 a sale** [C], before fees. If picks are not independent, this is too low.

**No subscription.** Dropped on 2026-11-09 (D-007). Users are meant to leave once hired.

**Per 1,000 people logging**, with 5% buying one $9 pack [A] at about $7.48 net [C], 40% hired [A], and $47.28 net per $49 [C]: if 25% of those hired pay, about **$5,102**, or $5.10 a user [C]; if 10% pay, about **$2,265**, or $2.27 a user [C]. $1M pre-tax in five years then needs about 196,000 users (3,270 a month) or 441,000 users (7,340 a month) [C]. The monthly $50 budget is covered by seven $9 sales [C].

**Employers (stage 3).** $299 × about 60 active roles × 60 months ≈ **$1.08M** [C].

## How the price was set

1. **Ceiling, from value.** At $60,000 a year [A], $58 equals about 2 hours of pay [C]. Value is not the limit.
2. **Floor, from cash.** The people using this are short of money, so the first payment stays under $10 and the larger one waits until income returns.
3. **Simple by rule.** Free, one $9 pack, one optional $49. No tiers, no subscription, no countdowns.
4. **Guarantee.** Removes the fear of wasting scarce money. Expected cost about $0.96 a pack [C].
5. **Test before raising it.** See test 5 in [Experiments](../04-validation/EXPERIMENTS.md).

The psychology behind this (under-$10 pricing, paying when income returns, refunds lowering perceived risk) is from general knowledge and has not been tested on these users.

## Reels as a channel, not an income

1M views ≈ 2,000 clicks ≈ 40 buyers [A: guesses]. Approximate direct platform pay at 10M views, from creator-tool blogs: Instagram $0, Shorts $300 to $1,000, TikTok $2,000 to $14,000, long-form $30,000 to $100,000 [V, low confidence]. Video gets attention. Revenue comes from the product.

## Costs in the test phase

Domain about $12 a year. Hosting free tier. Video tools inside the $50 monthly cap [A]. Legal advice is the first real expense; budget it before going beyond the free report.

## What would break this

Paying share under 10% of hires, buyers not paying the $49 once hired, a refund rate above 25%, or a legal complaint that forces removal of reply rates.
