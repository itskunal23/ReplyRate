# Risks

| # | Risk | Likelihood | Impact | Response |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Empty at start: no data, no reason to log | High | High | Report needs no data. Start narrow: about 200 companies |
| 2 | Teal or Simplify copies it | Medium | High | Move first on data. Public index as brand. Promise on scores |
| 3 | Selection bias: angry people report more | High | High | Show samples, adjust, publish the method |
| 4 | Defamation or legal complaints | Medium | High | Legal advice before naming companies. Counts-only cards. Correction process |
| 5 | Fake or hostile reports | Medium | High | Sample minimums, fraud checks, later vouching |
| 6 | Users leave when hired | High | Medium | Pay when hired. Ask for final outcomes. Stage 2 and 3 revenue |
| 7 | It does not actually raise replies | Medium | Very high | Test 4. If false, the product claim must change |
| 8 | Reels do not perform | Medium | High | Three formats, 10 tries, then re-evaluate |
| 9 | Privacy: application data is sensitive | Medium | High | Minimum data. No identifiable data sold. Delete on request |
| 10 | Gmail access costly or refused | Medium | Medium | Manual logging first |
| 11 | Harm to anxious users | Medium | High | Guardrails in the spec. No guilt mechanics |
| 12 | Founder time: not actually passive | High | Medium | State it plainly. Automate after the test |
| 13 | Name unavailable | Medium | Low | Check before buying anything |
| 14 | A legitimate repost gets read as a fake job, or a job feed changes its terms | Medium | High | Factual wording only, legal review before showing real company names, check each feed's terms |
