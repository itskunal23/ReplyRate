# Experiments

Thresholds are set before running. Do not move them after seeing results.

## Test 1: Ghosted Report

- **Question:** will people make and share the card?
- **Setup:** a free one-page tool and 10 Reels over two weeks. Counts stored in aggregate. No accounts.

| Measure | Pass | Fail |
|---|---|---|
| Reports made | 500 or more | under 100 |
| Share rate | 10% or more | under 3% |
| Added company names | 20% or more | |

Between pass and fail: change one thing and run it once more.

## Test 2: Tool

- **Question:** will people keep logging, and will any pay?
- **Pass:** at least 200 people log 5 or more applications, and at least 10 pay.
- **Fail:** under 50 log.

## Test 3: Hand-made offer (fallback before building)

- 100 job seekers, $9 hand-made best bets.
- **Pass:** at least 10 pay and half are still logging. **Fail:** under 3 pay.

## Test 4: Does it actually work?

- Compare human reply rates for people following best bets against a comparison group, matched on role and level.
- **Pass:** a clear lift with a stated confidence interval. The most important test of all, and the one a competitor cannot easily fake.

## Test 5: Price

Run after test 1 passes.

1. Ask at least 100 people who log the four Van Westendorp questions: too cheap, a bargain, getting expensive, too expensive.
2. Sell the pack at $9, $14 and $19 in alternating weeks with a real checkout, so nobody sees a different price on the same day.
3. Keep the price with the highest net income per 100 people logging, as long as refunds stay under 25% and logging does not drop by more than 10 points.

$14 beats $9 only if it keeps 63% of the buyers; $19 needs 46% [C].
