# Product thesis

## What the founder wants

- A side business that one person can run, that scales fast and could reach $1M or more.
- A consumer tool people badly want, spread through short-form video, with little other marketing.
- Jarvis, a voice assistant, tracking everything.
- Limits: $0 to $100 to start, $0 to $50 a month, no public founder persona.

## The honest tension

"Passive" and "fast to millionaire" do not hold together. A two-sided data product is real work until the data exists. Automation can take over the operations, not the judgment. Paths to $1M below are arithmetic on assumptions, not forecasts.

## The thesis

Job seekers are spending their scarcest resource, hours of hope, with no feedback on where it pays off. Employers carry no cost for silence. Whoever collects actual outcomes can do two things no résumé tool can: tell a person where their next application is worth sending, and make silence visible.

## Who it is for

Anyone applying for work and struggling, from a first job to late career. See [Audience](AUDIENCE.md). Reply data still needs a dense starting point, so it begins with about 200 widely applied-to companies. Laid-off US tech workers are the likeliest first contributors, because they apply at high volume across a small set of employers. That concentration matters: about 200 companies and about 6,000 outcomes gives roughly 30 outcomes each, which is a margin of about 18 points [C]. That is the smallest set that says anything useful.

## What must be true

1. People will report outcomes in exchange for seeing outcomes. [H]
2. The Ghosted Report spreads on its own. Target: every 2 users bring 1. [H]
3. Choosing employers by reply rate gets more human replies than not. This is the most important one, and it needs a comparison group. [H]
4. Job seekers will pay small one-time amounts. [H]
5. Selection bias can be corrected or disclosed well enough for the numbers to be fair. [H]

## End goal

Three stages, each earned by the one before.

| Stage | What exists | How you know it is time to move |
|---|---|---|
| 1. Tool | Report, logging, reply rates, daily best bets | People still log after 30 days. Enough outcomes per company |
| 2. Vouching | Each member can vouch for up to 3 people. A trust graph weights reports and opens warm paths | Reports are trusted, and fraud is the limit on quality |
| 3. Employers pay | Employers pay to learn why they lose applicants and earn a Reply Pledge badge. Later a Real Hiring Index | Employers contact us first |

In the end state a job seeker knows where to spend the next hour, and an employer has a reason to reply. **Employers can never pay to change a score.** If that line moves, the product is dead.
