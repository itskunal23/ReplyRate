# User guide

Open the link. There is nothing to install and no account to make. Applications are saved to that device and browser.

## The three steps

1. **Search a company.** See its real job posts and how often each has been reposted.
2. **Log where you applied.** Tap +, type the company, save.
3. **Apply where people reply.** Today shows up to three recent jobs that have not been reposted. When those are done, you're done.

On a hard day, just do step 1.

## Every action

| I want to | Do this |
|---|---|
| Check a company | Type its name, tap Check. It looks at job boards then, which can take about 30 seconds |
| Open a real job post | Tap its title on the company page, or Open job on Today |
| Log an application | Tap +, or I applied beside a job post |
| See where to apply today | Open Today |
| Watch a job | Company page, tap Watch. Your list is under You |
| See what changed | Open Today. Reposts of watched jobs and newly opened rates are at the top |
| Mark a reply | Applied, then Got reply |
| Stop waiting | After 30 days of silence, tap Stop waiting on Today |
| See closed applications | Applied, then Graveyard |
| Make my invoice | Enter your numbers, tap Copy card text |
| Get 10 best bets | You, then 10 best bets. $9, not on sale yet |
| Say I got hired | You, then I got hired. Paying $49 is optional |
| Delete my data | You, then Delete my data |

## What the numbers mean

- **Reply rate:** the share of applicants who heard from a human. Opens for everyone at 30 reports. A report is an application with a human reply or 30 days of silence.
- **Job links:** open the employer's site or a job board. Some boards block direct visits, so those jobs open a web search.
- **Reposted:** the same title and place, posted again on the same board at least 7 days later. It can be normal.
- **Ghost Index:** applications with no human reply after 30 days. Published once 200 are tracked.

No streaks, and nothing needs doing daily.
