# Community proposal (not decided)

The idea: a Yik Yak-style anonymous board for job seekers. Recommendation: **not an open board. Structured notes on each company instead.**

## Why not an open anonymous board

- Yik Yak grew fast, then faced [cyberbullying and threats that led to school lockdowns](https://ebsco.com/research-starters/business-and-management/yik-yak). It [shut down in 2017](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yik_Yak) as use fell.
- Posts here would name real employers. A US judge [ordered Glassdoor to identify reviewers](https://www.shrm.org/topics-tools/news/talent-acquisition/glassdoor-ordered-to-out-anonymous-users) in a defamation dispute, so "anonymous" can't be a promise. [Another court refused](https://www.workplaceprivacyreport.com/2016/01/articles/social-networking/attempt-to-unmask-anonymous-online-reviewer-denied/) on different facts.
- One founder cannot moderate an open board, and an empty feed looks dead at launch.
- The spec rules out endless feeds.

## What to build instead, in order

1. **"What happened" notes** after a logged application: stage reached, days waited, one short tip. Pseudonymous, tied to a real logged application, no naming individuals, report button and a correction route for employers. Shown after 5 notes at a company.
2. **Topic rooms by career stage** (first job, laid off, returning), at stage 2, with entry by vouching. [Blind](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Blind_(app)) shows the value of verifying who is posting.

Trigger: after test 2 passes and a lawyer has reviewed the terms.
