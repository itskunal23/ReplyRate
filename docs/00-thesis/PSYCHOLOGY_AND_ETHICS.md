# Psychology and ethics

The people using this are anxious and short of money. The founder asked for something people are obsessed with. The version we will build earns repeat use by being useful, and does not use the Instagram mechanics. Everything below is a hypothesis to test. The psychology is from general knowledge and has not been checked against primary studies.

## Principles we use

| Idea | Mechanism | In the product |
|---|---|---|
| "It's not you" | Comparing against a real baseline reduces self-blame | Your reply rate beside your field's. Shown only with real data |
| The Graveyard | Open loops keep draining attention; closure ends them | After 30 days of silence, you can lay an application to rest |
| Shared unlock | People work harder as a shared goal gets close (goal gradient), and it builds the dataset | Each company shows its real report count out of 30. At 30 the rate opens for everyone |
| Job watching | Real news brings people back without nagging | A watched job appears on Today only if it is reposted or its company's rate opens. No streaks, no daily prompts |
| Shared identity | "The Ghosted" turns private shame into a group | Monthly public Ghost Index |
| Earned hope | Hope works when it follows evidence | Your list changes when real replies arrive, and says why |
| Finish well | The ending shapes the memory of an experience | Each session closes with one next step |
| Humor as dignity | Reframing a loss as someone else's debt | The Ghosting Invoice |

## What we refuse

| Do not | Because |
|---|---|
| Fake percentiles or counts | It is lying, and the product is only worth its numbers |
| Fake scarcity or countdowns | Manipulates people short of money |
| Guilt notifications | They already feel bad |
| Punishing streaks | Missing a day of a job search is not a failure |
| Endless feeds | The goal is to get hired and leave |
| Selling identifiable data | Breaks the reason anyone reports |
| Letting employers pay to change a score | Destroys the product |

## On "addictive"

States and families are suing social apps over addictive design ([example](https://fortune.com/2026/01/27/instagram-tiktok-youtube-finally-face-day-in-court-over-whether-they-peddle-addictive-products-to-kids/)). Beyond the ethics, it is a poor fit: this product succeeds when a user stops needing it. The growth loops that remain are honest ones: a card worth sharing, data that improves with use, and a monthly public number.

## Interface principles

- Clarity: one primary action per screen, large titles, system type sizes.
- Deference: content first, translucent bars, restrained color.
- Depth: pushed detail views and bottom sheets show where you are.
- Touch targets at least 44 points. Tab bar with 5 items at most. Light and dark. Reduced motion honored.
- Status color never stands alone: always icon or words too.
