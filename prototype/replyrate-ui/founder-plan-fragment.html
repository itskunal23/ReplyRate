Where it’s going

  1. The tool

    The report, logging, reply rates and daily best bets, open to anyone applying, with reply data starting at about 200 widely applied-to companies.

    Next stage when most people still log after 30 days.

  2. Vouching

    Each member can vouch for up to three people, which makes fake reports harder.

    Next stage once reports are trusted.

  3. Employers pay

    Employers learn why they lose applicants and can earn a Reply Pledge. A Real Hiring Index comes later.

    Starts when employers contact us first.

The goal is a job market where employers answer applicants, because their reply rates are public.
